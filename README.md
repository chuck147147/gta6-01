## PALMERA BAY

An original top-down 2D open-world crime game built with native JavaScript ES modules and Canvas, with zero libraries. Explore a coastal city’s five districts—Coral Heights, Old Port, Marina District, Palm Heights, and Neon Mile—and landmarks including The Gilded Palm casino and The Lighthouse. Travel on foot or drive six car types through AI traffic and pedestrians, with a 0–5-star wanted system, police pursuit, and BUSTED/WASTED outcomes.

Work for fixer Delia Voss through a six-mission story chain: a pickup, timed delivery, tailing, car theft, a casino score, and a finale. Earn extra cash with repeatable taxi fares and hunt 24 hidden golden records; spend it on repairs, respray, snacks, and armor. A live minimap HUD, day/night cycle, synthesized WebAudio sound, and localStorage saves round out the game.

- WASD / arrows: move or drive
- Shift: sprint
- E: enter / exit car
- Space: handbrake
- F / click: punch
- M: mute
- Esc: pause

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080 (the game also runs offline by opening game/index.html in desktop Chrome; ?seed=6 picks the city seed and ?shot=chase|foot|mission|night poses a scene).
